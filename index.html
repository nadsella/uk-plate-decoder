Age identifiers

Since 2001 the two digits change twice a year. In March they match the year (25 = March 2025). In September they add 50 (75 = September 2025).

Memory tags

The first letter is the region. The second letter is the local DVLA office it was allocated to. I, Q, U and Z are never first letters.

Office names are the original 2001 allocations. Most DVLA local offices closed in 2013, so newer plates show the region they were allocated to rather than where the car was sold.

This tool reads the format only. It doesn’t look up a vehicle’s make, tax or MOT. For that, use the DVLA vehicle enquiry service.
